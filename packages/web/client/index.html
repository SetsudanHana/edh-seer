What this does

EDH Seer reads the oracle text of every card in a Commander decklist and reports which pairs actually work together — with the reason printed next to each one. It is not a popularity list: nothing here is derived from how many decks run a card.

How it reads a card

A language model is used once, offline, to turn printed card text into structured clauses — a trigger, an effect, a subject. Everything after that is ordinary code: deterministic rules match what one card produces against what another cares about. No model runs when you analyse a deck, which is why the same decklist always gives the same answer and why the whole analysis happens in your browser.

What an edge is

One card supplies an event, another card triggers on it. Impact Tremors cares about creatures entering; Krenko, Mob Boss makes Goblin tokens; the edge between them is that supply meeting that demand, and the report says so in a sentence you can check against the two cards. A claim with no sentence behind it is a claim this engine will not make.

What it refuses to claim

Ramp, removal and card draw are roles, not synergies — they make a deck work without pairing with anything, so they are counted as slots rather than drawn as edges. A card that refers to itself ("this creature", "this spell") is read as itself and not as a class. Where the rules cannot say something, the report says nothing: a missing claim is better than a confident wrong one.
