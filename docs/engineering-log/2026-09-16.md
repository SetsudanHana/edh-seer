# 2026-09-16 — Four AF10 rulings, four widenings, and the one that had to be built three times

**The rulings (owner, one sitting, morning).** The recall-v6 draw of 09-10 left four questions
the judges could not settle. The owner answered all four, every one a widening: (1) a bare type
outside the whole board narrows a tutor and a recursion — asked for the third time, and the
answer came with its reason: *"the refusal was made because previously everything dying had this
edge"*, the dies-mesh reflex, not a judgement on the relation; (2) a token maker feeds an
each-player edict; (3) "a land card" put onto the battlefield reaches a Mountain demand through
the deck's own lands ("admit via deck lands"); (4) a class-restricted dig reaches the cards of its
class the way a class-restricted search does. Baseline on `main` that morning: edges 41,975,
reasons 56,181, MESHED 449, panel 99.3% / retention 92.0%, compass 41/55. Every PR left the panel,
the ratchet and the compass where they were, and no deck's top theme moved.

**Ruling 1 (PR #332, matcher).** `loneTypeNarrows`: every type in the subject and its `anyOf`
branches must sit outside `WHOLE_DECK_TYPES`. Mystical Tutor → Counterspell — the 09-07 tutor
ruling's own witness — was silent under the subtype bar until this; Fabricate → Sol Ring and
Buried Ruin → Scrap Trawler with it. Worldly Tutor, Demonic Tutor and Crucible stay silent. +1,781
edges, +2,773 reasons, and **MESHED 449 → 1,349** — larger than the 990 measured on 09-10 because
tutors fan out beside recursions (Inventors' Fair `tutor:artifact` ×63 next to Buried Ruin ×63).
The number was on the table when the ruling was made, and it stays on the roadmap line.

**Ruling 2 (PR #333, matcher).** The fodder pass read only `control: "you"` sacrifice emits.
"Each player sacrifices" derives `any` — and so do "target player sacrifices" (Twisted Justice) and
"each opponent sacrifices" (Vona's Hunger), which your token never feeds. 131 corpus creature
edicts carry `any`; the printed cue `EACH_PLAYER_SACRIFICES` is what separates Innocent Blood from
Wing Shards, the per-player phase template included (Mana Vortex, Molder Slug). +185 edges.
`pair-probe.ts` reads SILENT for Bitterbloom Bearer → Fleshbag Marauder, because the pair joins
through the token node; the card-page path (`tokensMediate: false`) is where the probe sees it.

**Ruling 3 (PR #334, matcher) — built three times, and the deck report is what refused the first
two.** The relation is a deck fact, like a chosen type: `deckLandTypes` reads the deck's land
subtypes (all, and basics alone), analyze.ts passes them through `ReasonOptions.landTypes`, and
`landPutFor` in the event loop resolves an authored untyped land `enters` emit against the subtype
the consumer's trigger names. The first shape rewrote the emit with an `anyOf` over every deck
subtype and **lost 89 edges**: the plain "any land" reading was gone, so a subtype-less land's own
ETB stopped being reachable from an untyped put. The second restored it with an empty first branch
and read as the producer's own entry — *"When Gerrard's Hourglass Pendant enters, Valakut deals 3
damage"* — and, worse, resolved a fetchland's own implied entry: *"When Bloodstained Mire enters,
Valakut deals 3 damage."* Neither was visible to pair-probe (no deck) nor to the unit tests (they
asserted tags, and the tags were right). A one-shot that prints every reason naming a card in a
calibration deck's report (rakdos-landfall runs Valakut) caught both. The third shape resolves per
consumer, one subtype at a time, only when the plain match fails, and excludes the producer's own
implied events by the identity `producerEvents` dedupes on; the sentence reads *"When a Mountain
enters thanks to PuPu UFO, Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle deals 3 damage."* Never written into the
tags: `themeSubjectKey` reads a subject's first `anyOf` branch, so a rewritten emit would have
re-keyed every ramp spell's landfall supply from `enters:land` to `enters:forest`. +13 edges — the
family on the 71 is Valakut in one deck (eleven new rows: PuPu UFO, Walking Atlas, Burnished Hart,
Fabled Passage, Solemn, Wayfarer's Bauble, Bitterthorn, Canoptek Wraith, Gerrard's Hourglass
Pendant, whose four-type "return … land cards" includes the Mountains that died this turn).

**Ruling 4 (PR #335, DERIVE 143).** The `put library → hand` row that was added and reverted on
09-07 comes back gated by `digNamesAClass`: a dig is a `search` only when its object names a type,
subtype, stat or name; "two of them" (Dig Through Time) and "a card" stay kindless, which was the
whole of the 09-07 evidence. Free re-derive of 32,317 docs; cards with clauses but zero abilities
2,774 → 2,751; theme-stats moved only `static:search` 2 → 4. Two matcher corrections rode along.
`keyedOn`: a subtype-LIST tutor is keyed on the member the found card carries — Eclipsed Flamekin
→ Smoldering Marsh is `tutor:mountain`, where `themeSubjectKey` alone took the first of the list and
reported the Marsh as an Elemental. And the first measurement at 143 put **Narset, Parter of Veils
at `tutor:-creature` ×63**: "a noncreature, nonland card" resolves to six concrete types, every one
outside the whole board, and ruling 1's gate read the list as a class. A negation names no class
(the rank `themeSubjectKey` already gives it); refused, mesh 1,349 → 1,518 → 1,349. +125 edges.

**Cumulative on the 71:** edges 41,975 → 44,079 (+2,104), reasons 56,181 → 59,422 (+3,241), MESHED
449 → 1,349; panel 99.3% / 92.0%, ratchet ok, compass 41/55 throughout.

**Two lessons.** A widening in the event loop is measured on a DECK's report, not on a pair: the
pair is right and the deck is wrong twice in one morning. And the mesh census is the number to
watch when a class gate opens — both wrong fan-outs (tutors at ×63, Narset at ×63) showed there
first and nowhere on the panel, whose pairs were judged before either existed.

**Left open (roadmap AG5, AG6).** Wording debt: a fetch's `anyOf` sentence names the FIRST branch
(*"When a Swamp enters thanks to Bloodstained Mire, Valakut deals 3 damage"* — the match was the
Mountain branch), and "can search up" on a dig is the sentence the owner objected to on 09-07,
now unavoidable once the kind is `search`. AF7 (b)–(f), AF9g and Gadrak's `cant` threshold stand.
Recall v7 is the measurement that would say what the four widenings did to verb-match; nothing on
the 71 can.
