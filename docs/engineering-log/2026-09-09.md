## Evening — the three recall-v4 leftovers, one PR each (#296, #297, #298)

All three turned out derive-side, and two of the three roadmap guesses about WHERE the defect sat
were wrong (#151 was called matcher-side; #28 was called a `flipPerspective` question).

**#120, a damage multiplier on fight damage.** The CR 614 damage template captured the VICTIM
("a permanent or player") as the trigger subject, because when it was written a damage emit
recorded only its object. Since 2026-08-27 an authored damage emit carries `GameEvent.dealer` and
`eventMatches` compares a damage trigger against the DEALER, so Fiery Emancipation demanded
`permanent` and Khalni Ambush's typeless fight dealer could never satisfy it — nor could any
authored damage emit satisfy any multiplier. The source phrase before "would" is now the subject,
the damage half of `restricted` is retired (Gratuitous Violence's "a creature you control" is a
demand `subjectMatches` checks on the dealer), and a fight's dealer is a `creature` (CR 701.14a).
Left open: a dealer carries no colours and not the card's own type, so Torbran's "a red source"
and a creature's own ping stay silent under a creature-only multiplier. DERIVE_VERSION 131: edges
40,656 → 40,729, reasons 54,285 → 54,373.

**#151, a DFC back face's type.** Scryfall files the fifteen Transformers under layout
`transform`, which `playableFaces` reads as front-face-only — right for a werewolf, wrong for a
card CR 702.162 lets you cast converted. The printed keyword "More Than Meets the Eye" is read off
`keywords`, the back face implies its own `cast` and `enters`, and Tails sees Starscream's Vehicle
entering. `goldfish.ts` keeps its own front-only rule for castability (CEILING). DERIVE_VERSION
132: edges 40,729 → 40,854, reasons 54,373 → 54,612.

**#28, an opponent's graveyard filled by an edict.** Two defects. Hellish Rebuke grants "sacrifice
this permanent" to PERMANENTS YOUR OPPONENTS CONTROL and derived it as the instant's own death:
`grantedRecipientOf` (PR #294) accepted only creature-shaped recipients, and its rewrite sat in the
general trigger branch, which a `damage-dealt` trigger never reaches. The recipient may now be a
permanent, the rewrite is shared by both branches, and it follows the EMITS and the granted "you"
(CR 113.8) — so Rebuke's `dies` is `{control: opp, type: permanent}` and its life loss is the
opponent's. Valgavoth derived NOTHING: its replacement is Leyline of the Void's sentence, refused
on purpose, and its "play cards exiled with" is a play from exile no verb names. A card printing
BOTH halves (Valgavoth, Dauthi Voidwalker — two in the corpus) is a static `graveyard-recursion`
over `control: opp`, the shape Memory Plunder already carries; Leyline and Anafenza still derive
nothing. DERIVE_VERSION 133: edges 40,854 → 40,930, reasons 54,612 → 54,703, and the panel lost
one FALSE claim (Rebuke's sacrifice feeding a recursion over YOUR graveyard): precision 98.7% →
99.0% on 439 claims.

## Last — the magnitude design, drafted for a ruling

Roadmap AE4, a spec and no code: `specs/2026-09-09-edge-magnitude-design.md`. The three 08-18
refusals all refused one term, the deck-relative supply:demand discount, and the diagnosis was
cross-role bleed — a deck-level denominator renormalises every card in the deck when it moves. So
the design's single constraint: every term is about the PAIR or the GAME, none about the deck. The
weight is a product, `KIND × RATE × REACH × SPECIFICITY`: KIND the prior we carry; RATE off the
producer's `Ability.repeats` × amount (once 1, per-cycle 4, per-turn 6, repeatable 3 as a ceiling
until the mana model prices it); REACH off the consumer's effect scope × amount; SPECIFICITY one
minus the demand class's share of the commander-legal corpus (creature 0.44, artifact 0.89, a
subtype about 0.98, a whole-deck demand floored at 0.05 — admitted at a twentieth, never refused,
which is what the owner asked for). It admits the creature and permanent board counts and Toski's
generic combat trigger at low weight, drawn only above a floor; it keeps the land count and real-card
creature fodder refused on the standing rulings. The measurement is the 08-05 shape: the owner ranks
sixty payoff-with-three-feeder triples blind, the engine's orderings sealed in the key, and the rule
— ship if the product's pairwise concordance beats today's weight by non-overlapping intervals and
no gate moves — is registered before the draw. Six rulings are listed for the owner; nothing is
built until they are given.
