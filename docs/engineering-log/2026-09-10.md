# 2026-09-10 — Edge weight is the deck's strategy, and nothing else

**The ruling that ended the product (owner, 2026-09-09 night, after draw v1 scored).** *"We are
overcomplicating this. If I am an enchantress deck I care more about enchantment edges; if I am an
artifact deck, I care more about artifact edges. That should be as simple as that. With this we are
mixing up EDGE impact vs CARD impact."* The §2 product was withdrawn — not because it lost draw v1
by one pair, but because it answered the wrong question. Two quantities had been folded into one
weight. EDGE impact is how much a relation matters TO THIS DECK: an edge on the deck's strategy
matters, an edge off it does not, and that is the axis boost that already ships
(`1 + 1.5 × axisWeight`), which on draw v1 was the best single term (60–63% concordance against
58.7% for what shipped). CARD impact is how much the feeder DOES — an engine against a body — and
RATE and REACH belong there, on the card, re-parked as roadmap Y9. What shipped was edge impact
diluted by three card-shaped priors (KIND, repeatability, scaling), and removing KIND was the one
ablation that had helped. So the implementation of the ruling is a subtraction: every reason
weighs 1, and the deck's axis is the whole edge weight. Spec §8.

**The switch, then the gate, then the ship (PRs #309, #310).** `impact-weights.json` carries
`edgeModel: "priors" | "strategy"`; one guard in `impactWeightOf` returns 1 under `strategy`. #309
shipped it at `priors` — byte-identical to before — so the two models could be read on the standing
gates before any default moved. At `strategy` on the 71: rank-inversions 1,017 → 463 (clears);
protected majority-payoff headline falls 164 rows over 147 cards, which the gate as written calls a
failure. Split by whether the card's RAW score fell: **7 raw falls**, every one a per-permanent or
unbounded scaling multiplier (1.5–2.5) leaving the edge — Ovika (`cast:-creature`), Urza Lord High
Artificer twice (`scales:artifact`), Burakos (`scales:cleric`), Vren (`scales:rat`), Mass Appeal
(`scales:human`) — and **156 renormalisations**, the protected card rising and another card rising
more (Urza's Saga 8.84 → 10.02 raw in colorless-honest-magic while Surge Conductor takes the top).
Ratings moved 4,864 of 6,571, top card 31 of 71 decks, top-10 membership 67 of 71, anchoring up
nearly everywhere (bello-enchantress 4 → 5, braids 3 → 4.7). The biggest risers are on-axis payoffs
whose KIND was priced low: Prowl 2.4 → 5 in two decks, Niv-Mizzet the Firemind 1.38 → 4.02 (`damage`
0.2), Chandra's Incinerator 2.4 → 5, the five rakdos-landfall landfall bodies 2.4 → 4.6 as a block.
The 08-18 gate was registered against a DISCOUNT — a term that could only lower a protected payoff
— and under a re-weighting the deck max moves, so a deck-relative rating falls while the card
gains; the gate cannot decide this. The owner read the 42 top-card moves and the 7 raw falls and
ruled: *"I would ship strategy."* #310 flips the default, deployed from main the same morning — the
static manifest stays `v-ed3813b54d19` by construction (it hashes card data; deck analysis runs in
the client bundle), and the served `api.static` chunk on edhseer.cards reads the new key. A first hand-run on 09-09 night had set
every prior to 1.0 in the file and read 846 / 221; it left `UNKNOWN_KIND_WEIGHT` (0.2) on a reason
with no effectKind, which is not the ruling, and those numbers are superseded.

**What the ruling did to the tests.** Two analyze tests asserted exactly what the ruling removes: a
draw payoff out-scoring a damage payoff with the same partners (the 2.1 mis-ranking), and a
per-creature scaler out-ranking a fixed twin. Each now runs two arms — under the shipping model the
pair scores the SAME, because what separates them is a card question — and the old ordering is
kept under an explicit `edgeModel: "priors"` so the priors' arithmetic stays proven.
`SEED_IMPACT_WEIGHTS` keeps `priors`: the seed IS that arithmetic and calibration's regularisation
target; what ships is the committed JSON. Suite 3,888 pass / 8 skipped. Panel and compass are
structurally unreachable by a weight (nothing a claim reads changed), as the 08-18 record
established; the 60 draw-v1 rankings stand as the sanity check (STRATEGY alone ≥ today, in-key).

**What stays open, stated.** SPECIFICITY (a subtype against "creature") is not in the ruling —
whether a whole-deck demand is admitted at all is a TRUTH question per refusal, measured on its own
PR. Card impact (RATE × REACH, rate propagation) is Y9, ranks CARDS within a deck, never edges. Draw
v2 as registered is withdrawn.

## The recall-v5 families, six PRs after four rulings

**The nine of fifteen v5 misses that share two mechanisms** — a class named on the grantor's side
of a grant, doubler or copier; and a zone fill whose consumer reads a class the fill cannot promise
— were taken one PR at a time, each measured on the 71 with panel, population and compass, each
pair checked in isolation with a new one-shot, `research/instruments/pair-probe.ts`. The owner
ruled four questions in one sitting first: a grant to a TYPE-narrowed class is admitted, extending
the typed-tutor ruling of 09-07; a typed recursion joins the CARDS of its class and the fill side
stays refused (a creature edict still cannot promise a Vampire); a -X/-X or -N/-N sweep on all
creatures emits `dies`, control as printed; a free attach (Mithril Coat onto a legend) is enablement
and not an edge, which keeps judging ruling 4. Baseline on main that morning: edges 40,233,
reasons 53,516, meshed 289, panel 99.3% / retention 91.5%, compass 41/55.

**Evoke sacrifices the creature (CR 702.74a, PR #312).** Ashling, the Limitless never saw an
evoked Ingot Chewer because the keyword line reaches derive as `none` with its reminder text
stripped. The first cut derived the sacrifice as an authored ability and the pair stayed silent —
an authored self emit carries no type, and only self `leaves` and graveyard fills get the card's
types stamped. The matcher's keyword-to-implied-events table already turns `echo` and `cumulative
upkeep` into a typed self `sacrifice` + `dies`, so evoke is one row there. +30 edges; 35 cards, 20
decks, sixteen of them Fury.

**A doubler records WHOSE triggers it doubles (PR #313, derive 136).** `doubles` held WHICH event
(Panharmonicon's "entering") and recorded on 08-22 that WHOSE ability was a different axis with no
slot; Twinflame Travelers ("a triggered ability of another Elemental you control") sat on it with
seven others. `doublesOf` is the slot, read off the printed frame and refused for the whole board
(Mirror Room) and attachments (Wizard's Staff); the matcher pairs the carrier with every class
member that has a trigger of its own, one claim per consumer. The probe caught Roaming Throne
doubling every creature in isolation — a chosen type is a deck fact, so it is resolved in the deck
pass and refused unresolved. +78 edges, +199 reasons, Harmonic Prodigy in six decks.

**A type-narrowed grant keeps its recipient (PR #314, derive 137).** Cybermen Squadron's
"nonlegendary artifact creatures you control have myriad" reached nothing because the grant gate
admitted only a subtype, a commander or a token class. `boundedGrantClass` adds legendary, a
conjunction of types (`allTypes`, which `subjectMatches` reads as AND) and a lone type outside the
whole board; state narrowings ("attacking", "tapped", "equipped") and "nontoken creatures" stay
refused — the ruling named nontoken, and it is left out on purpose as the whole board minus tokens.
+372 edges; MESHED 289 → 449, every new row K-9, Mark I reaching 55 and 53 legendary creatures in
the two legends decks that run it. That is the ruling's own fan-out, the same kind cost-reduction
was admitted at on 08-18; it was not added to the census's exemption set, which is a separate call.

**A sweep debuff emits dies (PR #315, derive 138).** "All creatures get -X/-X" derived `debuff`,
correctly not an anthem, and emitted nothing, so it filled no graveyard while "destroy all
creatures" always had. The reviewer caught "-1/+1" reading as a sweep; tightening to two negative
halves then refused four real sweeps — Deluge of Doom, Terror Tide, Essence Pulse, Dead of Winter
— whose -X/-X the clause layer writes as "-X, where X is …". The rule is "starts negative and has
no positive half". +314 edges, +545 reasons. Toxic Deluge now feeds Blood Artist, Animate Dead and
Valgavoth; **Espers to Magicite is still silent**, because its reflexive copy-a-creature-card-from-
an-opponent's-graveyard derives as a bare token maker with no graveyard demand.

**A typed recursion pairs with the cards of its class (PR #316).** Bloodline Necromancer to each
Vampire and Wizard, in the tutor pass's shape and at the tutor pass's bar — a subtype or a stats
predicate, plus legendary and a conjunction of types. **A bare type does not narrow, and this was
measured before it was decided**: with lone types admitted, "return target artifact card" reached
62 cards in each artifact deck (Buried Ruin, Myr Retriever, Trading Post, Scrap Trawler) and the
mesh census went 449 → 990; at the tutor's bar it is 449 → 449. Final +354 edges, +641 reasons. Sun
Titan reaches Sol Ring on its mana-value predicate, the Imperial Recruiter admission. **Lara Croft
to Mithril Coat is still silent** for a different reason: her exile-and-replay derives as
`graveyard-hate`, not as a recursion.

**"You descended" is a fill demand (PR #317, derive 139).** CR 700.11: a permanent card into your
graveyard from anywhere. An intervening if forms no edge (owner, 2026-08-20), so Scalding Tarn → Tunnel-
Grinder stays refused by design; the condition now cares `dies` / `mill` / `discard` /
`enters-graveyard` `:any`. Inert on the 71 — ratings moved 0 — with the tags on the row.

**Cumulative on the 71: edges 40,233 → 41,351, reasons 53,516 → 55,374, MESHED 289 → 449; panel
99.3% / 91.5% unchanged and the ratchet ok on every run; compass 41/55.** theme-stats regenerated
(only `dies:creature` moved, 2,657 → 2,760). What the day did NOT close, listed on roadmap AF7:
the take-from-a-graveyard-and-use consumers (Espers, Lara), an `exiled` verb for the fourteen
processors, a graveyard threshold on a spell (See Double), three singles, and two wording debts —
a self sacrifice reads "When X sacrifices something", and the fill-enables-recursion sentence names

## Recall v6: the families closed, and what was under them

The closing draw for the day's six PRs: seed 20260911, 201 rows, three blind judges, the v5 rubric
plus the morning's rulings. verb-match 11.9% → **16.4% [9.4, 27.1]**, derive-empty 3.0%, base 4.5%;
pooled recall **94.9%**, up from 92.6% and the highest yet. The intervals overlap, so the verb-match
number is a direction, and the honest reading is that 16.4% sits inside the v3 / v4 band (17.9 /
16.7) and v5's 11.9% was the low draw. What did move is the population: the day's +1,118 edges were
STATICS — grants, doublers, typed recursions — and the stratifier's verb read never files a static
as verb-match, so verb-match lost 88 pairs while base lost 916. The families landed in base, and
base is where the rate fell.

The prediction on record was "the two v5 mechanisms shrink; the residue is AF7's shapes." Half
right. A class named on the grantor's side drew zero of sixteen (it was four of fifteen); a fill
whose consumer reads a class it cannot promise drew two, and both are refusals the owner chose — a
bare-type recursion (Buried Ruin → Scrap Trawler, the AF5 measurement), and "a land card" put onto
the battlefield into Valakut's Mountain demand. But the residue is not AF7's take-from-a-graveyard
consumers (one of sixteen). It is a layer under the families: how derive reads a COMPOUND subject,
and who controls what. Every miss was re-run in isolation with `pair-probe.ts` and every one is an
engine silence, then traced to the clause, the derived abilities and the matcher pass.

Nine are defects and none needs a ruling. Two disjunctions collapsed to conjunctions: Canoptek
Spyder's "another nontoken artifact creature or Vehicle" derives its `anyOf` correctly and then
`allTypes: [artifact, creature]` on top of it, so a Vehicle that is not a creature never matches;
Skullport Merchant's "sacrifice another creature or a Treasure" derives a creature with subtype
Treasure, which nothing is, so a mana-value-2 Zombie is neither fodder nor a fed death trigger. Three
controllers read wrong: Morbid Opportunist's "whenever one or more other creatures die" carries
`control: you` from the CLAUSE — the normalizer's error, four corpus cards, and a derive-side read
of the subject text covers it without a re-buy — so nothing an opponent loses feeds it; Braids' "you
may sacrifice" emits as the opponents' sacrifice; Lazotep Quarry's "exile target creature card from
your graveyard" emits as an opponent's and as graveyard-hate. Gadrak's "a Treasure token for each
nontoken creature that died" gives the Treasure `type: creature`. Eclipsed Flamekin's "look at the
top four, reveal an Elemental, Island, or Mountain card, put it into your hand" is in the clause and
derives only the bare enters trigger. And two board-count gates are one notch too narrow: Blight
Pile counts creatures WITH DEFENDER and the pass refuses `creature` as the whole board without
reading the keyword; Nettlecyst counts "each artifact and/or enchantment" and the pass requires
exactly one type. One shape is new and inexpressible today: an Aura goes to the graveyard when its
host is sacrificed (CR 704.5m), which is Chime of Night fed by Dockside Chef, and no card writes
that emit. Three rulings go to the owner, one of them for the third time: whether a bare type
narrows a recursion or a tutor.

## AF9: the v6 defect queue, five PRs the same evening

The nine defects recall v6 found under the families, taken one mechanism per PR and measured on
the 71 decks each time. Baseline after the morning: edges 41,351, reasons 55,374, meshed 449,
panel 99.3% / retention 91.5%, compass 41/55.

**Two board-count gates one notch too narrow (PR #321, matcher).** The battlefield-count pass
admitted a subtype or exactly one non-whole-deck type. Blight Pile counts "creatures with defender"
— `creature` plus a keyword, and the type alone read as the whole board; Nettlecyst counts "each
artifact and/or enchantment" — a type disjunction, and the gate demanded one. A keyword narrows the
way a subtype does; a disjunction whose every branch is outside the whole deck is narrower than
either board, while "artifact or creature" is still the board. `subjectMatches` already read both
fields. Edges +117, mesh unchanged.

**A cross-slot OR is a disjunction on every path (PR #322, derive 140).** "Another creature or a
Treasure" puts a card type on one side of the "or" and a subtype on the other, and the two fields
are ANDed downstream. The disjunction (`anyOf`) was built on the trigger path only, and even there
the outer subject kept `allTypes` while the branch lost it, so Canoptek Spyder's "another nontoken
artifact creature or Vehicle" demanded an artifact creature of every Vehicle; the emit path used a
bare `parseSubject`, so Skullport Merchant's sacrifice derived a creature with subtype Treasure,
which nothing is. Now inside `parseSubject` itself. Two things the first cut got wrong and the
measurement caught: three removal spells went to zero abilities because the permanent-shaped test
read only the outer type (Bounce Off, Gravkill), and the reviewer found that where the OR cannot be
split — inside a relative clause, "target creature that blocked or was blocked by a Zombie" — the
invented AND came back; the old fallback drops the subtype and does again. Edges +54; the 253
zero-ability cards with an OR in a subject are the same 253.

**The controller is read off the printed text, not the clause's guess (PR #323, derive 141).**
Morbid Opportunist's "whenever one or more other creatures die" carried `control: you` from the
normalizer with nothing printed behind it, so Feed the Swarm and Braids fed it nothing. The clause's
`you` is right for an actor verb ("whenever you cast a spell" normalizes to subject "a spell"), so
the flip to `any` is confined to permanent-event verbs, to class subjects, and to a printed trigger
phrase with no "you" or "your" in it — read up to the comma that ends it, skipping the commas a list
subject carries of its own (the reviewer's Valley Mightcaller). 67 corpus triggers flip, 1,415 keep.
Braids' "you may sacrifice X. If you do, each opponent may sacrifice" has one sacrifice action and
the actor cue handed it to the opponents; your own verb, written first, keeps it. Lazotep Quarry's
"exile target creature card from your graveyard. Create a token that's a copy of it" is recursion,
not hate and not nothing, and its target is yours — and the same rule closes Lara Croft's
exile-and-play, the AF7a leftover. Found underneath: `isSelfSubject` knew only the first face's
short name, so "Whenever Venom attacks" on Eddie Brock // Venom had never been self; about 17
triggers on 14 double-faced cards now are. Edges +531, retention 91.5% → 92.0% (Braids → Chainer
and Liliana's Triumph → Morbid Opportunist recovered, banked).

**A count is a magnitude, not a class (PR #324, derive 142).** Gadrak's "create a Treasure token
for each nontoken creature that died this turn" read `type: creature` off the count. `parseControl`
has cut at the count cue since roadmap I2; the class words now cut there too, and a subject that is
only a count names no class at all (the reviewer's "X, where X is the number of Elves"). 30 deck
cards change, every one dropping a leaked class — Aetherflux Reservoir's lifegain, Blast Zone's
sweep, Comet Storm's damage. Edges +39. Gadrak → Treasure → Swashbuckler is claimed through the
token node, a path the card-to-card probe cannot see. theme-stats regenerated: 55 keys gone, every
one a leaked count's class — `draw:spell`, `gain-life:elf`, `counter-added:food` — and 103 counts
moved; a key that has left the corpus but stayed in the stats would score maximum idf and win every
ranking, which is why the regeneration ships with the derive.

**One "defect" was a standing refusal.** Eclipsed Flamekin's dig ("look at the top four, reveal an
Elemental, Island, or Mountain card") derives nothing on purpose: `effect-kind.ts` records a `put
library → hand` row added and reverted on 2026-09-07 — a dig four cards deep cannot find the piece,
so it is not a copy of it. The v6 judge read ruling 3, which is about a search, onto a dig. It goes
to the owner as a fourth ruling. The Aura-dies-with-its-host shape (CR 704.5m) was counted and left
open: 113 corpus Auras, 7 in the 71 decks, most of them the "enchanted creature dies" shape the
engine already reads.

**Cumulative: edges 41,351 → 41,975, reasons 55,374 → 56,181, meshed 449 unchanged; panel 99.3%,
retention 91.5% → 92.0%, debt 8; compass 41/55.** Two process lessons, both paid for: never switch
branches while a derive, a measurement or a reviewer is running, because the working tree is the
code being measured — one checkout put a wip commit on the wrong branch and inflated a reading by
exactly the other PR's delta; and `pair-probe.ts` is card-to-card and reads a token-mediated pair as
silence.
