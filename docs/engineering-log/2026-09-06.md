# State as of 2026-09-06

## The archetype vocabulary is EDHREC's theme list; detection stays ours

Owner, the same morning: *"we are short of the themes ... there are strategies like Theft, Big
Mana, based on mechanics like Party or Dungeons"*, and the ruling that followed: *"edhrec right now
would be only the way to define those, the recognition of if deck is XYZ we have to do ourselves
based on our engine."*

The detector had 13 members, mirrored by hand in three classifiers (`archetypes.ts`,
`theme-names.ts`, `MECHANISM_CATEGORIES`), and the owner had found the same hole in each of them one
at a time (PRs #104, #111, #112). The vocabulary rule that binds `TRIGGERS`/`VERBS` — size against
what the game expresses, never against the decks in hand — binds the archetype list too, and the
population's own taxonomy is the honest size reference.

**The dictionary.** EDHREC's `pages/tags.json`, 401 themes with deck counts, fetched the same day.
Every one classified on purpose, with a generator that refuses any slug landing in two places or
none:

| class | rows | examples |
|---|---|---|
| strategy | 87 | Control, Theft, Big Mana, Blink, Storm, Wheels, Extra Turns |
| mechanic | 94 | Cascade, Party, Dungeon, Cycling, Ninjutsu, Day/Night, Speed |
| type | 21 | Equipment, Vehicles, Sagas, Rooms, Shrines, Lands Matter |
| resource | 12 | Treasure, Food, Clues, Energy, -1/-1 counters |
| kindred | 1 member, 135 tribes | one parametrised row; Dragons … Crocodiles map onto it |
| excluded | 52 | named-card builds (Rat Colony, Cheerios), roles (Ramp, Counterspells), format labels (cEDH), 60-card slang, Attractions/Stickers (legality) |

"Tribal" is Kindred — the type was renamed (CR 205.2a), and the owner corrected the word.

**Detection.** A member is DETECTED when `ARCHETYPE_SIGNATURE` has a row, DECLARED otherwise
(owner's choice: option 1, "declared, not detected" — no owner-set strategy, no build-shape
heuristic; A17 already refused the interaction count for Control). 121 members detect, 94 are
declared and the file lists the signal each one waits for, because every one is a normalize
vocabulary gap and not a matcher patch: no `gain-control` verb reaches the corpus (Control Magic
derives `enters:aura` and nothing else, so Theft cannot be seen), no `x-cost` kind (Big Mana),
no counter kind (-1/-1 rides on wither/persist; charge, oil, rad, energy are blind), and the theme
key keeps a subject's type but drops its controller ("each opponent" and "you" read alike, so
group slug, group hug and self-damage are blind). Of the owner's four: Dungeon detects (37 corpus
cards print "venture into the dungeon"), Party, Theft and Big Mana are declared.

The grammar grew four fields, each earned by a row that could not be written without it:
`keywords` (printed Scryfall keywords, 791 distinct in the corpus — the named mechanics have no
derive signal, the keyword IS the membership; evergreen ones never, 2,143 fliers would clear the
floor in every deck), `lineWords` (type-line words for the object classes), `tokenKinds` (the
resource rows), `allTags` (a wheel is a discard AND a draw on one card) and `demandTags` (the
payoff a keyword row has no supply tag to name). Every signature's tag verbs and effect kinds are
checked against the tagger vocabulary in the test, so a row keyed on a signal the derive layer never
emits — the silent wrong answer — fails.

**Kindred is the type the PAYOFFS name, not the one the bodies share.** Humans are on 2,761 corpus
cards; twenty incidental Humans beside twelve Elves and three Elf lords is an Elves deck. The lord
is the payoff every kindred deck is built around and it is an EFFECT subject, which the cares tags
never carry — so `CardSignal.namedTypes` reads the subjects of every ability. No payoff, no row,
same as Enchantress. The first run led four Voltron decks with "Kindred: Equipment" (a reconfigure
creature is an Equipment), one with "Kindred: Food" (Gingerbrute): candidates are now filtered to
creature subtypes via the tagger's `SUBTYPE_TYPES`.

counter kind that would separate them is not derived); Graveyard with `graveyard-recursion` topped 7
of 10 Reanimator decks. A row that is a superset of another always outranks it, so
`counters-matter` is declared and Graveyard keys on the keywords that cast from the yard plus the
enters-graveyard payoffs only. Wheels ungated flipped four owner decks on their loot spells (a
spellslinger at 27%); gated on a draw/discard payoff, those four are back and Bumbleflower and
Kefka read as Wheels instead, which they are.

## The panel's last 4%: families 1–3 fixed, four pairs banked

## The template is the theme's own row: rulings, the file, and what it moved

**The owner ruled on the four open questions of the proposal** (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-06-theme-template-proposal.md` §6), the same afternoon: *"median, 0.6 I think is ok, well lands calculateable based on your cmc, land strategies can boost the requirement, 4 makes sense."* So: the theme's median, not a higher quantile; a secondary at 0.6 of the primary's confidence, blended by confidence-weighted mean; lands stay Karsten from the deck's own curve with a land theme's delta on top (`landfall: { lands: 4 }` is the one delta left in `ARCHETYPE_TARGET_DELTAS`); the report shows the rows. Wipes were not ruled on and keep the proposal's shape — the population's 2, a row only where a theme leans by more than one.

**The file.** `packages/matcher/src/template-targets.json`, regenerated by `research/template-fit.ts` from `packages/cli/decks/edhrec/`: 212 theme rows over 2,126 decks (goodstuff skipped on purpose — its population is cEDH midrange; the one vocabulary member without a directory is Kindred, 135 tribes, unpulled), a `population` row of 13 / 11 / 13 / 2, and a `range` per parent that the ratchet (`template-targets.test.ts`) holds every value inside. 23 themes carry a `boardWipes` key. `BUILD_PARENTS` now reads its four fallback targets from that `population` row; the Command Zone's 14 / 10 / 10 / 3 is gone from the code, and with it every hand-written role delta (voltron +3 protection, combo +4 tutors, tokens −2 wipes, superfriends +3 wipes — the last is now a row, 6.5). `templateBlend` (build.ts) is the rule from spec §3.2 verbatim: `strategies[0]` leads when it clears `ARCHETYPE_LEAD_FLOOR` (0.25) and has a row, `strategies[1]` joins at ≥ 0.6 of its confidence, the target is the confidence-weighted mean rounded to the half, and a deck with no theme strong enough scores against the population.
