**FOUR FIXES, EACH WITH THE THING THAT MADE IT NON-OBVIOUS:**

- **`control: "any"` is a PERMISSION, not a neutral unknown** (2c1d1e4). `matcher/subject.ts:39`
  skips the control check whenever EITHER side is `any`, so a phrase `parseControl` cannot read
  becomes a wildcard. "defending player controls" fell through, and Mjölnir's tap satisfied Hawkeye's
  "whenever Hawkeye becomes tapped". The family is **12 clause actions, not the 333** an oracle-text
  grep suggests; all 12 mean an opponent. "that player controls" is REFUSED on purpose (antecedent,
  not a role) with a test pinning the refusal. Panel 83.7% → 84.1%, false 73 → 71, **no real claim
  lost**. Expect this asymmetry wherever a parser can return "unknown".
- **The theme line never read the stats it was handed** (0c59087). `analyze.ts` fed `themeStats` to
  `buildAxis` and ranked themes with `UNIFORM_STATS`, where `globalIDF` is `log 2` for every tag —
  ranking collapsed to raw frequency and seven of eight decks themed "draw". Compounded by
  `gen-theme-stats.ts` reading the FLAT `cardTags` collection: the committed artifact described
  N=20,410 flat-era cards, and an ABSENT tag scores `log(N+1)`, the MAXIMUM, so `lose-life:opp`
  (absent) dominated. **Neither fix is visible alone.** `hierarchy.json` shape again.
- **A structured theme names its mechanism, never its subject** (38e5248). `BARE_LABELS` is the FLAT
  vocabulary, so `enters:wizard` rendered as "enters" — not one of five tribal decks named its tribe.
  Rendering-only, zero tag churn. Measured and REJECTED first: composing legendary/historic into
  `themeSubjectKey` costs **22 judging debt and does not change the theme**, because the label drops
  the subject regardless. The tag key was never the lever.
- **A deck is what its payoffs CARE about, not what its cards do** (8ccda1d). `cardThemeTags` counts
  triggers, emits and statics alike, so 20 removal spells draining incidentally outvoted 7 magecraft
  payoffs. A trigger tag now counts full, a supply tag `PRODUCER_SHARE = 0.35`. Consumer-ONLY was
  measured and rejected — it strips wick-changelings of its rats and marchesa to `enters:any`.
  `computeCohesion` keeps the RAW deckFreq so the score stays readable; `buildAxis` untouched.

**THREE OPEN FINDINGS, each with a measured witness, none started:**
- **Extort derives nothing, and a CONSUMING keyword has no path at all.** 17 corpus cards, 3 derived,
  0 with a cast trigger. `keywordEvents` maps a keyword to EMITS (cycling, echo, cumulative upkeep);
  Extort TRIGGERS. Architectural, not a table row.
- **Subsumption only knows `:any`.** `cast:instant` ⊂ `cast:-creature` — the `-creature` type list
  literally contains instant and sorcery — so one ~9-card spellslinger theme ranks as two (30.4, 11.9).
- **One trigger with a chain of effects scores once PER EFFECT.** Charitable Levy shows four
  byte-identical sentences for one Arcane Signet. `score = reasons.length`, so it inflates rankings
  everywhere. Undiagnosed as right or wrong.
