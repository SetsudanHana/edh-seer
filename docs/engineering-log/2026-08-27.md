**A MULTI-FACE CARD IS TWO NODES NOW, NOT ONE — faces-as-nodes shipped 2026-08-27, free, a
nine-task plan, MEASURED IN THIS COMMIT AND CORRECTED IN A SECOND PASS THE MEASUREMENT ITSELF
DEMANDED.** Owner's request, on the Jodah board: *"those should be 2 separate nodes in the graph,
cause if you flip the card it can care about different events and produce different ones."* Every
printed face of every multi-face card (transform, modal DFC, adventure, split, prepare) is its own
node in `unique`, the pair pool, the ratings pass and on the board — a face with nothing to say
still gets one, marked by a shared rim, no link between them. `resolved` (the 100 physical cards)
is untouched: a reason still NAMES the physical card and carries `producerFace`/`consumerFace`
beside it. **`report.cards` — the Cards table, the CLI's ranked list — IS THE RATINGS PASS, so it is
per FACE now too**: a two-faced card prints two rows, each carrying `cardName` (the physical card
both rows belong to, set on BOTH faces) and `face` (the printed face index, absent on the front) so
a reader can join a row back to its card without a special case. The cut list merges faces back
before proposing anything — you cannot cut half a card.
