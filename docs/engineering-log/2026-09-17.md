# 2026-09-17 — "You draw" is a stated actor (DERIVE 152)

**Owner-reported off the new 60-row pages:** Scrawling Crawler ("whenever an opponent draws a
card, that player loses 1 life") listed Y'shtola, Night's Blessed, whose draw is yours — "if a
player lost 4 or more life this turn, YOU draw a card". The draw derived `control: any`: the
recipient reader fills `you` only when the sentence names no player at all, and this sentence
names "a player" in its condition. `any` meets `opp` in the matcher, so every "whenever an
opponent draws" payoff was fed by half the card-draw in the corpus. Census before the fix: 671
draw emits at `any`, ~350 of them printing "you draw" or a bare "draw N cards" with no other
player on the verb.

**The fix, in `actionRecipients`:** a printed "you <verb>" is a stated actor for that verb, not an
unstated one — it outranks a player word elsewhere in the sentence and yields only to an opponent
cue on the same verb written before it (the Braids rule: yours first, then theirs, stays contested).
Two things the first derive-152 reason diff caught: (1) inside a GRANTED ability a stated "you" is
the recipient's controller — Hellish Rebuke's "You lose 2 life" is said by the opponent's permanent,
and the actor cue ran after the flip and undid it; the actor now maps through `grantedTo`; (2) "you
may put up to one target creature card from THAT PLAYER's graveyard onto the battlefield under your
control" (Sepulchral Primordial, Ink-Eyes) wrote `you` onto the recursion subject and turned it into
a recursion over your own graveyard — 10 pairs lost; a stated "you" on a put reaches the entry
emit and never the subject.

**Measured on the 71:** edges 45,107 → 45,085 (−22), reasons 61,102 → 61,072; 31 pairs lost, every

Crawler, Faerie Mastermind, Orcish Bowmasters, The Unagi; Shadow's "you may sacrifice" → Kamber,
Yahenni, Legate Lanius; Oblivion Sower's lands under YOUR control → Sire of Stagnation), 8 gained

99.3% / 92.7%, ratchet ok; compass 41/55. Draw emits at `any` 671 → 552, at `you` 2,927 → 3,062.
CEILING: only the verb "you" sits against is stated — "you draw a card and lose 1 life" states the
draw's actor and not the loss's, which still falls to the sentence default.

**A counter put on the card itself is a self emit (DERIVE 153).** Owner-reported next: Primal
Amulet listed as feeding Exemplar of Light's "whenever you put one or more +1/+1 counters on THIS
creature, draw a card". The Amulet's charge counter lands on the Amulet. An add-counter's object in
the clause layer is the COUNTER ("charge counter", "+1/+1"), never the permanent receiving it, so
the emit builder could never see "this artifact" and only 81 of the 4,321 corpus counter emits
carried `self` — every self-growing creature "fed" every own-counter payoff (Exemplar, Danny Pink,
Dusk Legion Duelist, Evolution Witness, Fathom Mage, Blaster). The recipient is in the clause text:
`counterOnSelf` reads "on this <type>", "on <its own name>", and "on it" only when nothing else in
the clause could be "it" (a self trigger, or "This creature enters with … on it"); adapt and
monstrosity are self by rule; a clause naming both a target and the card claims nothing. The
matcher stamps the card's own types on a self counter emit (`selfLeavesTypes`), so the Amulet's
counter is an ARTIFACT's and refuses "counters on a creature you control" by type, not by luck.

Two things the first derive-153 diff caught. The Great Henge's "whenever a nontoken creature you
control enters, put a +1/+1 counter on IT" read as self and unjoined the panel's Henge → Dusk Legion
Duelist REAL pair — the ratchet fired, which is what it is for; "on it" now needs a self trigger or
the enters-with shape. And stamping types moved a self counter with no recorded KIND onto the strict
path, where a consumer's `+1/+1` against an absent kind read as a mismatch and Shelinda, Yevon
Acolyte stopped feeding Simic Ascendancy; the kind is compared only when both sides state one.
