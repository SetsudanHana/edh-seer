**A CARD IS CAST AND PLAYED ONE FACE AT A TIME** — FIXED, DERIVE_VERSION 30. `splitTypeLine` unions
every face's types. That is correct for what a permanent can BE (a transformed Westvale Abbey really
is a Demon, and a consumer watching creatures should see it) and wrong for what ENTERS or is CAST,
because those happen to one face. Read as one merged subject, "Instant // Land" is a land you cast
and an instant that enters the battlefield — neither of which is a thing.

`Characteristics.faces` lists the faces a card can be PLAYED as, unmerged, and `impliedEvents` is its
only reader; `types`/`subtypes` stay the union for everything asking what the permanent can BE.
A **transform or flip** card lists only its front — the back is reached by transforming a permanent
already in play, which is not a zone change (transform 393 · flip 26). A **modal DFC, adventure,
split or `prepare`** card lists every face, because each really is castable in its own right
(98 · 158 · 135 · 52). `FRONT_FACE_ONLY` must stay an ALLOW-list, never a reject-list, or the next
layout printed gets silently narrowed.

**and were denied a `cast` entirely**, because the union said "land" and lands are not cast; Dion //
Bahamut was keyed `cast:enchantment` when you cast a Legendary Creature; Fell the Profane // Fell
Mire supplied nothing to any spellcasting payoff; Witch Enchanter claimed a LAND attacks.
Power/toughness are stripped from a non-creature face (Marang River Regent's Instant half was
advertising power 4). **`cmc` is NOT split** — face mana costs are on the card document, not on
`Characteristics` — so an adventure's spell half still reports the creature's mana value.

**chosenType rubric: judge the choice against the deck, not the card alone.** Never mark a chosenType
edge `uncertain` just because the choice is nominally free. Three cases:

2. **Multi-type payoff** — Voja, Jaws of the Conclave counts Elves for counters and Wolves for draw.

`media.wizards.com/<year>/downloads/MagicCompRules%20<YYYYMMDD>.txt`, plain text, no PDF extraction.
Rule **122.1b** fixed the keyword-counter dictionary (a18a969): it had an invented `ward` counter and
was missing decayed/exalted/shadow. Use it for any closed list that is a RULES concept — MTGJSON and
Scryfall cover card data, neither enumerates counters.

**A COUNTER IS A BOARD STATE, NEVER A PRINTED CHARACTERISTIC.** Populating the channel broke three
gates at once, all comparing a counter against a card's type line: `counterAddMatches` (an
add-counter's subject is parsed from "+1/+1" and names no type), the static applies-to pass, and the
self-trigger identity gate. All three now strip it, exactly as the self-recursion gate already
stripped `zone`. The panel caught all 8 lost real claims.

**`legendary` is now a real filter** (09ce98d). The two widest `static:pump` meshes were
legendary-matters anthems (Serah Farron, Jodah) reading as EVERY creature, because supertypes had no
filter. Modelled on `historic`; 45 cards name legendary in a subject. Set on BOTH sides —
`characteristicsSubject` AND `selfSubject` (implied.ts) — because the first cut gave consumers a
legendary demand that a legend's own entry could not satisfy, and silently cost 5 real edges.

**WIDTH IS NOT EVIDENCE OF A MESH.** Serah x53 / Jodah x51 survive and are correct: they are
legendary commanders whose decks genuinely run 50+ legendary creatures. Twice in one session a
"widest mesh" turned out to be ordinary bugs or correct behaviour, never the deck-role question I
guessed. Read the card.

**An edge is binary; synergy has MAGNITUDE.** Every creature in a deck feeds Prowl's "another
creature enters", but a token engine (Aragorn, Ellie, Eggman) feeds it many times, Lord Skitter's
Butcher twice, and a plain body once. Ranking by edge count cannot say which card is the reason the
other is in the deck. `reason.repeatability` and `effect.scaling` already exist and are the material
to build on. Scoring change, needs brainstorming — not a defect fix.

**`Ability.repeats` answers "how often" and shipped 2026-08-11** (`packages/tagger/src/derive/
repeats.ts`, DERIVE_VERSION 32) — a DIFFERENT axis from the existing `repeatability`, which reads
`Ability.kind` alone, so Gogo, Master of Mimicry's `{T}` ability and a free sacrifice outlet are both
`"activated"`. Five labels: `once` (fires, then done) · `per-cycle` (~once/round — `{T}`, your
upkeep) · `per-turn` (~once/turn, so up to pod size a round — an opponent's upkeep, "each opponent's
second draw") · `repeatable` (unbounded within a turn) · `continuous` (a static, never fires, always
on) · unset (refused, not guessed). Weights live in `rules.json`, not the corpus, so retuning never
costs a re-derive.
**`Ability.cost` is now populated** (the resource ledger, below — 1,494 of 1,501 activated abilities
carry the real string). `repeatsFor` still does NOT read the field: it takes `cost` as its own
positional argument, threaded in separately from `segment()`, so populating `Ability.cost` cannot
move a `repeats` label or `REFUSED_CAP` — the two channels stay independent on purpose.

**Answer coverage now carries exile and recurring sub-counts, and both are MEASURED, not scored**
(2026-08-11, `specs/2026-08-11-answer-modes-design.md` §9). Owner's ruling: exile is the only
recursion-proof answer — a tuck is a draw away, a destroy is reanimator food, so "can't be
regenerated" and forced sacrifice don't count (sacrifice fills a graveyard, the opposite of

total count of 1–6, and over half the decks with artifact/enchantment/planeswalker/land removal
carry ZERO of it exile-shaped (creature is the outlier, under a third, being the widest class). On
graveyard hate, 55/71 decks carry some, only 8/71 carry hate that RECURS — 6 distinct cards
(Ghost Vacuum · Grafdigger's Cage · Leyline of the Void · Lion Sash · Soulless Jailer · Valgavoth,
Terror Eater — `·` as separator because that last name has its own comma). The doctrine's
prediction — recursion-proof coverage far thinner than coverage, recurring hate far rarer than
hate — held on both axes. `required`, `available` and `buildScore` are unchanged; `BASE_TARGETS.graveyardHate` is still
0, and moving it off zero is the next step this measurement anchors, not this one — it re-tunes
`buildScore` for all 71 decks and needs its own before/after. `answer:graveyard` grew 33 → 35 distinct
cards along the way (Grafdigger's Cage, Soulless Jailer) — the recurring rule sees static hate that
never says "exile", which the gate was amended to allow: answer classes may GROW with per-card
justification, never shrink.

**FINAL REVIEW FOUND 13 OF THE 58 THRESHOLDS WRONG, FIXED, DERIVE_VERSION 38.** `thresholdFor` read
`clauseTexts[clause.id]`, a whole `segment()` clause that routinely spans two or three printed
sentences, and returned the first qualifying comparison wherever it sat — including a comparison
that gates a RIDER, not the trigger. Primal Amulet's "put a charge counter on this artifact. Then if
there are four or more charge counters on it, you may remove those counters and transform it" put the
counter on REGARDLESS of the count; gating the counter-placement at 4 was a false sentence. New
**exclusion 3**: refuse a comparison after "Then", or outside the clause's first sentence. Both halves
are needed — Kuja, Genome Sorcerer is caught by "Then" only (its quoted granted ability leaves no
sentence period after `segment()` rewrites it), Omnath and Dowsing Device are caught by the sentence
test only. THE TRADE, accepted by the owner: the predicate refuses the whole clause, so ~7
currently-correct thresholds were lost along with the 12 wrong ones — a missing answer beats a wrong
one. Threshold count 58 → 38, exactly the predicted range. **A separate, PRE-EXISTING ceiling is NOT
fixed by this**: `trigger` is built once per CLAUSE and the same object is assigned to every ability
that clause derives, so Golbez, Crystal Collector's two genuine thresholds in one clause (4 artifacts
gates the return, 8 gates the life loss) still collapse onto one shared trigger. Left for sub-project
B — see the design spec §10.
Also fixed the same review pass: **`drainAbility` set no `cost`**, breaking the schema's own
"absent means not activated" invariant for its one corpus instance (Lampad of Death's Vigil,
`"{1}, Sacrifice a creature: ..."`) — cost coverage 1493 → 1494. `amount` stays unset on a drain: it
merges two source actions and no single amount is attributable.
**THE PARSE WAS EASY AND THE GATES WERE THE WORK.** 208 corpus sentences carry a numeric comparison
inside a condition; 98 are "one or more" (an English plural meaning ANY, not a count) and 29 are stat
comparisons already held by `SubjectFilter.stats`. Real population, **sentence-level** (regex matches
over corpus oracle text, pre-derivation, per the design spec's §6.1 method): **81** matches / ~76
cards, 3.0% — measured BEFORE the exclusion-3 fix above, so it still counts 13 comparisons that no
longer derive a threshold. **81 and 38 are not the same count measured twice** — one printed sentence
can segment into several `Ability` records that each inherit the same threshold (Twenty-Toed Toad's
"attack with two or more creatures, put a counter... and draw a card" is ONE sentence but TWO
abilities, both threshold 2, which is why its witness below returns `atLeast` three times not two),
and a trigger clause can derive zero abilities at all (the re-derive log reported 124 cards with
clauses but zero abilities) — so the ability-level count moves in both directions relative to the
sentence-level one and neither figure is wrong.
**A thousands separator parsed the headline witness as ZERO** — `\b\d+\b` matches "000" inside
"1,000", so The Millennium Calendar derived `atLeast: 0` until the number pattern became
`\d+(?:,\d{3})*`. Without the threshold the corpus reads Calendar as winning the turn it makes one
time counter.
**Twenty-Toed Toad's witness is `[2, 2, 20]`, not just the `20` the design spec's §7 table names.**
Verified against corpus oracle text: "Whenever you attack with two or more creatures, put a +1/+1
counter on this creature and draw a card" is a real board-state gate (2, correctly bucketed as
"other board count," not a stat predicate) that derives as two separate abilities
(`counter-placement` and `draw-card`) sharing one trigger, so its threshold appears twice; "you win
the game if there are twenty or more counters on it or you have twenty or more cards in hand" is the
third, `atLeast: 20`. Three thresholds from two sentences is correct, not a defect.
