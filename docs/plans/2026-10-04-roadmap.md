## The direction

1. **Trust before breadth.** The synergy sentence is the one thing nobody else does and it
   measures 98.7% precise. Four of six persona seats still take the page to a forum. Make the
   strong thing trusted on screen before adding to the page.
2. **One substrate.** Every engine downstream of derive (swaps, interaction, land facts, roles)
   reads the typed card reading, not its own regex. The grammar then pays out everywhere, and
   there is one classifier to measure.
3. **A panel for every engine before another feature.** Synergy has ground truth in the repo;
   swaps, interaction, mana and brackets do not. Labels first, then changes.
4. **Stop adding.** No new report chapters, swap kinds, bracket logic or persona seats until the
   first three hold.

## Deferred, on purpose

New report chapters; new swap kinds beyond role, land and synergy; more Game Changer upgrade
logic; more persona seats; the next set's bought answers for cards the grammar reads completely
(G-T1 makes that automatic). Each of these adds surface the rounds cannot yet trust.

## How progress is read

One number per week, from the instrument that fits the week:

- week 1: the persona round's component medians and tasks-answered, against 10-02;
- weeks 2 and 3: G1 purity on the swap labels, precision and recall on the interaction labels,
  the share of role swaps justified by mana alone (50% of uses today), the share of interaction
  cards with no kind (29% today);
- week 4 onward: the persona round again, the mana base pairwise panel, the plan-seeker verdict.

Every number is re-measured by the script named in its review and quoted with its date, as the
engineering log has always asked.
